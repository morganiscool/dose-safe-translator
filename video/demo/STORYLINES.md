# Demo film: storylines

Proposed Nov 15, 2026 (all about 35-45 s, a Blender opening, real screens, no voice):

- **A. Two boxes:** one counter, a US carton found by barcode and a box from another country read from
  its printed directions.
- **B. Before and after:** "No label found" again and again for a box from abroad, then the app naming
  the barcode, reading the directions and translating them.
- **C. One take round the box:** a single Blender camera move round one carton, the label unfolding from
  its barcode side and the printed lines lifting off its Directions panel.

**Picked:** none of these. The person asked for a film "just in general about how the app works and how
it affects people", so `SPEC.md` follows the patient and the pharmacist: the English-only label, the
risk of a free translator, then the app's three ways in (one line, the barcode, a box from another
country), ending on the slip the patient can read.
