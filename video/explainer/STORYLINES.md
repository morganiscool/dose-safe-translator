# Dose-Safe Translator explainer: three storylines

The one idea: a free translator can flip a medicine instruction, and a round trip plus targeted checks show a pharmacist which translations to double-check.
Shared assumptions: 40–55 s, 1920×1080, on-screen titles instead of a voice, Drug Facts look (carton blue #0e4c92, white panel, black bars, Public Sans).

## A. The label line's round trip (recommended: one real example worked through, which is what an explainer needs)
- **Device / form:** the worked example; an object-to-object chain. One real label line lifts off the carton and travels through every step, and each shot ends on the thing that opens the next.
- **What you see:** the Blender carton's Drug Facts panel, the camera on "do not take more than 6 caplets in 24 hours". The line lifts off and becomes Vietnamese, comes back as English with "more" turned into "less", the checks stamp Failed, the red verdict lands, and the patient slip prints.
- **First 2 s:** the carton turning, its Drug Facts panel filling the frame, the "6 caplets in 24 hours" line underlined in red.
- **Beats:** the label line → UCSF's "hold the kidney medicine" became "keep taking the medication" → Dose-Safe Translator → translate → translate back → nine checks → "more" became "less" → verdict and slip → "It flags what a person should check" → end card.
- **Look:** carton blue, white panel and black bars, the Blender carton and caplets.
- **Sound:** a quiet free music bed, with a click on each step and a rubber-stamp hit on Failed.

## B. A moment at the counter
- **Device / form:** a moment in someone's day; match cuts. The counter in 1950, then today, then the slip in the patient's hand.
- **What you see:** the 1950 Katz Drug Store counter (with its "Don't Take a Chance!" signs) matches into today's counter. A patient who reads Vietnamese, a carton, a free translator's wrong answer, then Dose-Safe catching it and printing the slip.
- **First 2 s:** the archival photograph slowly pushing in on "Don't Take a Chance!".
- **Beats:** the counter, then and now → the language gap → the old way fails → Dose-Safe's checks → the slip → the claim → end card.
- **Look:** black-and-white archival photographs meeting carton blue.
- **Sound:** a warm free music bed and store ambience.

## C. Six caplets (the bold one)
- **Device / form:** a number lands; one frame that rebuilds itself and never cuts.
- **What you see:** six Blender caplets in a row on carton-blue board under giant type, "no more than 6". The mistranslation flips "more" to "less", the row spills into many caplets, then the checks pull it back to six.
- **First 2 s:** the six caplets landing one by one, each with a click, as "6" lands in giant type.
- **Beats:** 6 → the flip → the spill → the round trip → the checks restore 6 → the claim → end card.
- **Look:** the Blender caplets, giant black type on carton blue.
- **Sound:** percussive clicks per caplet over a sparse free beat.
