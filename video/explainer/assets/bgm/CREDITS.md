# Music

`bed.mp3` is the first 45.3 s of a track from HeyGen's music library (search: "calm thoughtful minimal piano underscore"), retrieved with `media-use/audio/scripts/audio.mjs` while signed in to HeyGen, with a 0.5 s fade in and a 2.5 s fade out. Use follows HeyGen's terms for its music library. The full download (`track.mp3`) is not kept in git.
