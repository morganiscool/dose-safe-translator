Checks

    Photograph the barcode on a medicine box. Its official label is found, and these four parts of it are translated line by line and checked.

    Purpose
    What the medicine does, like “pain reliever / fever reducer”.
    Uses
    What the label says it treats.
    Directions
    How much to take, how often, and who may take it.
    Do not use
    When not to take it.

    Labels come from DailyMed (US National Library of Medicine), found through the FDA's openFDA database. Only the label's own words are shown and translated; nothing is added. Not every label lists its barcode there (16,218 of 49,694 over-the-counter labels did on Nov 15, 2026), so if yours isn't found, type the NDC number from the box.
